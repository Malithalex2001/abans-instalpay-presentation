1. Executive Purpose & Scope

This official evaluation document accompanies the live physical demonstration conducted for the leadership, Credit & Risk Committee, and IT Engineering department of Abans Finance PLC. It establishes verified proof of hardware-level locking, automated payment clearance, and enterprise security for smartphone financing across 400+ Abans showrooms.

2. Device Enrollment Pathways

To support all showroom operations, the platform provides two distinct deployment pathways:

Pathway A: Out-of-Box QR Provisioning

  • When Used: Brand new unboxed smartphones at showroom counter.
  • Execution: 6 quick taps on Android Welcome Screen $\to$ Scan Cashier QR.
  • Duration: < 60 Seconds end-to-end.
  • Privilege: Full Google Device Policy Controller (DPC) Device Owner.

Pathway B: Post-Setup APK Installation

  • When Used: Handsets already configured, pre-activated, or in-use.
  • Execution: Direct APK deployment with Device Admin & Accessibility sync.
  • Duration: < 90 Seconds without factory wipe.
  • Privilege: Secure Managed Device Administrator lock bridge.

4. Advanced OS Gateways (Zero-Touch & Samsung Knox)

For pre-provisioning factory-sealed shipments at the hardware IMEI level, the platform supports Google Zero-Touch Enrollment (ZTE) and Samsung Knox Guard/Manage.

6. Privacy & Security Governance (PDPA Compliance)

The platform operates under a Zero-Intrusion Privacy Model in full compliance with the Sri Lanka Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) No. 9 of 2022. The system strictly isolates device locking telemetry from personal user data and never reads photos, messages, browsing history, or call records.
