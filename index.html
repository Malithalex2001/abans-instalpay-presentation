Strategic Audit: SWOT Analysis

Internal technical defensibility alongside market expansion opportunities and risk controls.

STRENGTHS (Internal) S
  • • True Device Owner (DPC): Cannot be uninstalled; persists through factory resets.
  • • 30-Day Offline Policy: Monotonic hardware clock locks even with SIM removed.
  • • 3-Tier Gentle Escalation: Warning $\to$ App Restriction $\to$ Full Lockout.
  • • Emergency 112 Dialing: 112/119 national emergency pass-through always active.
WEAKNESSES (Internal) W
  • • Android Focus: iOS MDM policies require supervised configuration profile.
  • • Showroom QR Setup: Requires 60-second Wi-Fi scan during unboxing.
  • • Customer Contract: Financing terms must explicitly state lock criteria.
OPPORTUNITIES (Market) O
  • • Abans 400+ Showrooms: Immediate nationwide scale across retail & e-commerce.
  • • Smart TVs & Android OS: Extending device lock technology to any Smart TV or Android OS device.
  • • LankaPay Direct Debit: Automated recurring deduction via CEFT & JustPay.
THREATS (External) T
  • • Android OS Updates: Google Enterprise Partner compliance ensures longevity.
  • • Hardware Tampering: Knox KeyStore hardware attestation blocks modified ROMs.
  • • Data Privacy: 100% compliant with Sri Lanka Personal Data Protection Act.
