Phase 01 Showroom Wi-Fi Setup & How to Get the Correct SSID

Before onboarding customer devices, you must register your showroom's Wi-Fi network inside the Instalpay Backoffice. When you scan the enrollment QR code on a fresh smartphone, the phone will automatically read these credentials and connect to the internet with zero manual typing.

1
How to Find Your Correct Showroom SSID
Look at your showroom router label or check the connected Wi-Fi name on your cashier PC/laptop:
  • Exact Name (SSID): Must match letter-for-letter including capitalization (e.g., sltfiber or Abans_Showroom_POS).
  • Password: Enter the exact Wi-Fi password (case-sensitive).
  • Security Type: Select WPA (matches WPA / WPA2 / WPA3 on modern routers).
2
Save in Backoffice "Wi-Fi Setup" Tab
Navigate to backoffice.instalpay.lk → Click Wi-Fi Setup in the left sidebar → Enter your credentials → Click Save Wi-Fi Settings.
Figure 1: Wi-Fi credentials stored securely and bundled into all future enrollment QR codes.
⚠️ Important Mistake to Avoid (Captive Portal Networks):
Do not use public or guest Wi-Fi networks that require opening a web browser to enter a voucher code or click "Agree". The smartphone needs direct WPA2 internet access during initial setup to download the security suite silently.

Phase 03 60-Second Hardware Unboxing, 6-Tap QR & OTP Activation

Take the customer's brand new smartphone out of the box and execute the 6-tap Google Enterprise DPC handshake:

1
Power On to the Android Welcome Screen
Turn on the smartphone until you see the initial out-of-box Welcome Screen (displaying "Start" or "Hello" with the language selector).
2
Where to Tap 6 Times Rapidly
Tap 6 times in rapid succession anywhere on the blank white space of the welcome screen (in the center or right above the Start button).

The phone will play a confirmation beep and display: "Setting up QR code reader...", opening the camera scanner.
3
Scan the Onboarding QR Code
Point the phone's camera at the Onboarding QR Code displayed on your cashier computer screen.

How Long It Takes & Internet Speed:
  • Standard Wi-Fi (10+ Mbps): Takes 45 to 60 seconds to download the security suite.
  • Slow Wi-Fi (< 5 Mbps): May take 2 to 3 minutes. (Keep the phone near the Wi-Fi router).
4
One-Time OTP Pairing & Silent App Hiding
Once the DPC finishes downloading:
  • The app automatically verifies the agreement token. (If prompted, enter the 6-digit cashier OTP shown on your screen).
  • The phone registers its unique hardware IMEI with Abans Finance.
  • Automatic App Hiding: The Instalpay app icon instantly hides itself from the home screen and app drawer, running silently in the background as a Google Enterprise Device Owner service.
🛡️ What This Setup Permanently Prevents (Self-Secured Asset):
  • Factory Reset Blocked: Users cannot wipe the phone from Settings (DISALLOW_FACTORY_RESET).
  • Recovery Bootloader Wipe Blocked: Hard resetting via physical volume buttons triggers automatic re-lock upon first boot.
  • Safe Mode Blocked: Security policies cannot be disabled by booting into Safe Mode.
  • Developer Options & USB Debugging Blocked: Prevents computer hacking or ADB bypass attempts (DISALLOW_DEBUGGING).
  • Offline Autonomy: 30-day internal cryptographic clock enforces lock even if SIM is removed and WiFi is turned off.
📌 Pathway B (Post-Setup APK for Pre-Activated Phones):
If enrolling a customer phone that is already configured and in use, download the APK from backoffice.instalpay.lk/app and grant Device Administrator permissions in under 90 seconds without wiping user data.

Phase 04 Quick 48-Hour Temporary Unlock & Permanent Release

Special operational controls for customer service flexibility and final loan completion:

1
How to Grant a Quick 48-Hour Emergency Unlock
⚠️ Note: Only provided in Production when authorized by the showroom manager (e.g., customer promises in-person cash settlement within 48 hours or during bank gateway downtime).
  1. Log into Backoffice → Open Devices → Locate Customer IMEI.
  2. Click "Grant 48-Hour Temporary Unlock".
  3. The cloud sends a secure temporary token to the phone. The phone immediately unlocks for exactly 48 hours, returning to restricted mode if payment is not settled within that window.
2
How to Permanently Release a Phone (Loan Completed)
When the customer finishes their final installment (e.g. 6/12 months) or does a full early settlement:
  1. Record final payment in Backoffice → Agreement status turns to "Paid in Full / Completed".
  2. Click "Permanent Release / Remove Management".
  3. The cloud pushes an un-enrollment command → The DPC wipes all MDM restrictions → The phone becomes 100% clean, independent, and permanently unmanaged for the customer!

Reference Common Setup Errors & Cashier Troubleshooting

Quick solutions for simple mistakes during daily showroom operations:

Setup Error / Issue Root Cause Immediate Resolution
Phone doesn't beep after 6 taps Tapping too slowly or not on the welcome screen. Ensure you are on the very first "Start/Hello" screen. Tap 6 times in rapid succession in the middle.
Wi-Fi connection fails after QR scan Wrong SSID or password saved in Backoffice. Check Wi-Fi Setup in Backoffice. Verify spelling and case-sensitivity against showroom router.
DPC download hangs or times out Weak showroom Wi-Fi signal or slow internet. Move the device closer to the router. Ensure internet connection has at least 5 Mbps bandwidth.
Captive portal browser error Using guest Wi-Fi requiring web login. Switch showroom Wi-Fi to a standard direct WPA2 password-protected network.
Google Account FRP lock screen appears Phone was previously used and reset with Google account active. Enter previous Google credentials to bypass, or use Pathway B (Post-Setup APK Installation).
Paid at counter but phone still locked Phone was offline when payment was recorded. Connect customer phone to Wi-Fi/Mobile Data → In Backoffice click Force Unlock Sync.
