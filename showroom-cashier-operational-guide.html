Showroom Operational Guide: Customer Onboarding & 60s Phone Provisioning

A simple step-by-step cashier handbook for zero-risk smartphone financing across 400+ Abans Showrooms.
⏱️ Setup Duration: < 60 Seconds 📶 Wi-Fi Automatic Bundling 🔒 Google DPC Ownership 💳 Sub-30s Payment Auto-Unlock

Phase 01 One-Time Showroom Wi-Fi Setup (5 Minutes)

Before onboarding customer devices, configure your showroom Wi-Fi inside the Instalpay Backoffice. When you scan the enrollment QR code on a new smartphone, the phone will automatically connect to your shop Wi-Fi without typing passwords.

1
Open "Wi-Fi Setup" in the Left Sidebar
Log in to your showroom admin panel at backoffice.instalpay.lk and click on the Wi-Fi Setup tab.
2
Enter Wi-Fi Credentials & Save
Enter your showroom Wi-Fi network name (SSID) and Password. Select WPA as the security type and click Save Wi-Fi Settings.
Figure 1: Showroom Wi-Fi credentials bundled automatically into enrollment QR codes.
💡 Pro Tip for Cashiers: You only need to configure this once per showroom. All subsequent customer unboxings will connect automatically to this saved network!

Phase 02 Customer Registration & Installment Plan (30 Seconds)

When a customer selects a smartphone for hire-purchase financing, create their agreement profile in three quick steps:

1
Click "+ Onboard New Phone"
From the main Dashboard, click the blue + Onboard New Phone button in the top right corner.
2
Enter Customer & Guarantor Details
Fill in the customer's Full Name, NIC Number, Mobile Number, and Residential Address. (Guarantor fields are optional).
Figure 2: Fast 3-step customer registration wizard.
3
Set Installment Terms & Generate QR
Click Next: Installment Plan, select the device retail price, initial down payment, and monthly tenure (e.g., 6 or 12 Months). Click Create & Generate QR.

Phase 03 60-Second Smartphone Hardware Enrollment

Now take the customer's new, unboxed smartphone and perform the 6-tap Google DPC handshake:

1
Power On the New Smartphone
Turn on the brand new smartphone until you reach the initial Android Welcome Screen (displaying "Start" or "Hello").
2
Tap 6 Times Anywhere on the Screen
Tap 6 times rapidly on any blank white space of the welcome screen. The phone will beep and launch the built-in Android Enterprise QR scanner.
3
Scan the Onboarding QR Code
Point the phone's camera at the Onboarding QR Code on your cashier computer screen. The phone will:
  • Automatically connect to your showroom Wi-Fi.
  • Silently download the Instalpay Device Policy Controller (DPC).
  • Register its hardware IMEI with Abans Finance backend.
4
Complete Setup & Hand Over Device
Tap Accept & Continue. The phone is now 100% active, protected against factory resets, and ready to be handed to the happy customer!
📌 Pathway B (Post-Setup APK Installation): If a customer is enrolling a phone that is already turned on and configured, simply download the Instalpay APK directly from backoffice.instalpay.lk/app and activate Device Admin permissions in under 90 seconds without a factory reset.

Phase 04 Payment Settlement & Sub-30s Auto-Unlock

How the system handles monthly installments and ensures seamless customer satisfaction:

1
Level 1: Gentle Reminder Alerts (App is 100% Hidden)
3 days before due date, the customer receives gentle notification alerts. The app remains completely hidden in the background and the phone is 100% usable.
2
Level 2: Direct Device Restriction (If Overdue)
If an installment is unpaid at the cutoff, the screen temporarily locks down access and displays balance details with two instant buttons: Pay Instantly and Call Support.
3
Sub-30s Instant Auto-Unlock
The customer can pay via:
  • Showroom Cashier POS (Cash / Debit / Credit Card)
  • Online Payment Gateway on their locked phone
  • LankaPay CEFT / JustPay direct bank transfer
The moment payment is confirmed, the system sends an instant webhook and the phone unlocks in under 30 seconds automatically with zero staff effort!

Reference Cashier Troubleshooting & Emergency FAQ

Situation Immediate Cashier Action
Phone doesn't beep after 6 taps? Make sure you are on the very first "Start/Hello" screen. Tap quickly 6 times in the middle of the screen.
Wi-Fi connection fails during QR scan? Check that your shop Wi-Fi credentials under Wi-Fi Setup match your router password exactly.
Customer paid at counter but phone is still restricted? In Backoffice, go to Devices, find the customer's IMEI, and click Force Unlock / Refresh Sync.
Customer wants to change their SIM card? The customer is completely free to use any SIM card (Dialog, Mobitel, Airtel, Hutch). Lock protection stays fully intact.
